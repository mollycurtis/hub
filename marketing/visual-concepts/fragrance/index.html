Spec Campaign · The Brief

Selling a scent through a screen

SAHAR is a self-initiated campaign concept for a niche gourmand fragrance. The challenge was making a new scent feel desirable before anyone could smell it.

The challenge

Launch an unknown fragrance into a crowded luxury category with no celebrity face and a modest budget, where most shoppers discover new scents online and can't try them first.

The audience

Fragrance lovers in their mid-20s to early 40s who choose niche over designer, research scents through reviews and creators, and buy into story and ritual more than logos.

The insight

People don't buy notes, they buy a moment. The quiet hour before dawn is a feeling almost everyone knows, and no fragrance owns it yet.

The positioning

SAHAR, Arabic for the hour before dawn, is the scent of quiet ritual: warm, intimate and unhurried. Every touchpoint translates smell into light, texture and memory.

Creative Direction

Why it looks this way

Light

Low, warm side light and long shadows recreate the hour before dawn, so every image reads as the same quiet moment.

Texture

Smoke, skin, silk and resin stand in for the notes. When a fragrance can't be smelled online, texture has to do the selling.

Palette

Amber, espresso and deep plum come from the ingredients and the pre-dawn sky, and stay consistent across product, social and print.

Type

A soft italic serif carries the intimacy of the scent, and Arabic calligraphy honors the meaning behind the name.

Go-To-Market

How it would launch

01 · Tease

Two weeks of smoke, dawn light and skin close-ups on Instagram and TikTok, with no product shown, to build curiosity around the hour before dawn.

02 · Launch

Reveal the bottle with the hero image, send discovery samples to niche fragrance creators, and lead with a sample-first offer so new customers can try before they commit.

03 · Sustain

Invite customers to share their own hour before dawn, then retarget engaged viewers with display and paid social built from the sensory lines.

How success would be measured

Sample requests and sample-to-full-size conversion, engagement on teaser content, creator reviews and customer posts, and growth in branded search.
