Hi, I’m Molly.

Photographer, marketer, and the friend with the good eye, in one person. Based in St. Paul, working across the Twin Cities.

I have spent my whole career making things look intentional. As a marketer I decide what a thing should feel like and make every detail agree. As a photographer I read light and composition for a living. Styling a room is where those two habits finally meet.

My real obsession is the hunt: the back corner of the thrift store, the Marketplace listing with the terrible photo hiding a great chair. Most of what I use is thrifted, sourced or already yours, so budgets stay sane and your home never looks like a showroom. The point is never a specific palette. It is that your home ends up feeling like you.
