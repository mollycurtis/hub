Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This site is my personal portfolio. I collect as little as possible, I do not sell anything about you, and there are no ads or advertising trackers anywhere on it.

Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which work they look at. It sets cookies in your browser and collects things like the pages you open, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device and browser you use. Google shortens visitor IP addresses and I never see yours.

I only look at this as totals, to understand what is working. I never use it to identify anyone. If you would rather not be counted, you can install Google's opt out add on or use your browser's private mode or tracking protection.

Contact forms

When you send an inquiry, the form passes your name, email and whatever you write to Formspree, which delivers it to my inbox. I use that information to reply to you and to keep track of the project. I do not add you to any mailing list and I do not share it with anyone else.

Other services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and keeps standard server logs.
  • Cloudinary delivers the photos and videos.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.

Each of these may log basic request details, such as your IP address, as part of delivering the page to you.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser at any time, and the site will still work. If you would like me to delete an inquiry you sent, just email me and I will remove it.
